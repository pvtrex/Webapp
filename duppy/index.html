Frequently Asked Questions

What is open box product?

An open box product refers to an item that was returned by a customer, inspected for quality, and deemed fit for resale by the retailer or manufacturer. These products are typically unused or lightly used, come in their original packaging, and are sold at a discounted price compared to brand new items.

What is the return policy?

Our return policy allows you to return products within 7 days of purchase for a full refund or exchange, provided the item is in its original condition and packaging. Please refer to our detailed return policy page for specific terms and conditions.

Do you have delivery in all places or only in particular locations?

We offer delivery services across India, ensuring that our products reach customers in a timely manner. However, delivery availability may vary for remote or specific locations. Please contact our customer service for more information on delivery to your area.
